How to interpret payout ratios

Below 100% of positive earnings can be easier to sustain, but no single threshold determines dividend safety. Earnings may differ from cash available for distribution. REITs and financial companies often require different measures, such as AFFO or statutory capital rules.

*Free cash flow is only computed if operating cash flow and capital expenditures refer to the same SEC fiscal period. The entered annual dividend may describe a different period from the historical EPS, so any payout ratio is a comparison proxy, not an official current payout ratio.
