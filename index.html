*Increases/decreases follow the selected comparison period, using the report closest to the target date within 120 days (or the earliest/latest records for All available). When a historical baseline is missing, no change is shown; for 1 year or Latest reports only, a separately labeled third-party YoY estimate may be displayed. Comparison periods are anchored to each ticker's latest reported date, not today's date. Snapshot values are rounded and dated when checked, not necessarily on the filing date. Company changes may reflect offerings, buybacks, splits, and conversions. Fund changes commonly reflect creations/redemptions and must not be interpreted as shareholder dilution. A negative change does not independently prove a repurchase. Some ETFs, foreign issuers, and crypto assets have no comparable SEC company share-count disclosure. Scheduled checks may be delayed by GitHub Actions. Market prices are separate historical end-of-day closing prices, not live quotes or exact trading prices; compare dates carefully.
